Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

1. Introduction

At TickleApps, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, websites, and services provided by TickleApps, including NutriScan.

2. Data Collection & Use

We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as device identifiers, usage statistics, and diagnostic data to improve our products. We do NOT collect personal information like your name or email unless you explicitly provide it to us (e.g., through a support request).

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve ads on our platform. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

4. Firebase & Analytics

Our mobile applications use Firebase, a service profile by Google, for analytics and crash reporting. Firebase helps us understand how our apps are being used and identify performance issues. This data is anonymized and used solely for product improvement.

5. User Consent & Third-Party Advertising

By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. Third-party advertising partners may also collect data through the use of device identifiers and cookies to show relevant ads.

6. GDPR & Global Standards

We follow GDPR-friendly language and practices. If you are a user in the European Union, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete any data we may have collected. Contact us at support@tickleapps.com for requests.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@tickleapps.com.
